# Lead Registration Page

A responsive, static lead registration page deployed on Vercel.

## Lead delivery
Submissions are sent to **magsonfernandes@gmail.com** through FormSubmit's AJAX endpoint. On the first submission, FormSubmit may send an activation email to the recipient inbox; confirm that email before expecting ongoing lead notifications.

## Deploy
This is a static HTML site. No build step is required.

## Privacy
The page collects only the fields shown in the registration form. Do not add sensitive personal or financial information to the form without an appropriate privacy and retention policy.
